# DiskDuster App - Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 5, 2026

## Summary

DiskDuster does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your Mac.

## Data Collection

**We collect nothing.** DiskDuster does not:

- Collect personal information
- Transmit data over the internet
- Use analytics, crash reporting services or tracking
- Communicate with any external servers

## What DiskDuster Reads

To find reclaimable files, DiskDuster reads folder names and file sizes in a fixed list of locations, such as `~/Library/Caches` and `~/Library/Logs`. It does not read the contents of your documents. Scan results are kept in memory only and are discarded when you quit.

## Data Storage

DiskDuster stores its settings, including any paths you choose to always ignore, locally in your user preferences. Nothing leaves your Mac.

## Changes

We may update this policy occasionally, but will never snoop on you or collect analytics. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version.
